Is the wedding monogram generator really free?

Yes. Create your monogram, download the outlined SVG and share a clear-ice preview for free. No signup is needed, and your design stays in your browser.

Can I use the SVG with another stamp maker?

Yes. The download is a physically sized, black-on-white vector SVG with outlined lettering. Share it with your maker and confirm stroke widths, gaps, mirroring and production tolerances before machining.

Which diameter should we choose for wedding ice?

Start with 2.0 inches (50 mm nominal) for wedding monograms. Measure the flat face of your actual cube. A 1.5-inch (38 mm) stamp suits simpler initials; a 2.5-inch (63 mm) design needs a larger cube than a standard 2-inch cube.

Does the ice preview guarantee a readable impression?

The preview is a concept image. The readability pill estimates stroke and subtitle clearance at your chosen size; Studio checks the rasterized geometry. Actual results depend on ice clarity, temperature and technique. Confirm a workshop proof before machining.
