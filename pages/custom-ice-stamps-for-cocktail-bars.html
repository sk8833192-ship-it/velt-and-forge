Service protocol

Built into the station,
not added to the ticket time.

A stamp only earns its place if it keeps pace. This is the routine we recommend training on before the first shift.

  1. 01

    Set the station.

    Clear ice in a tempering tray, the stamp on a dry room-temperature plate, a clean bar towel within reach. Never store the stamp in the ice well.

  2. 02

    Temper, press, lift.

    Let cubes sit 3–5 minutes until the frost turns glassy. Press straight down for about 2–3 seconds (brass) or 4–5 seconds (steel), then lift straight up.

  3. 03

    Wipe between rounds.

    Towel-dry the face every few cubes so meltwater doesn’t pool in the relief. Wash, sanitize and dry at close.

Before the

Lead-free bismuth brass conducts heat quickly, so it marks in roughly 2–3 seconds on tempered ice, which helps during a rush. 304 stainless steel has a bright brushed finish, no brass patina and is easy to wash and sanitize, but needs about 4–5 seconds. Many programs use brass at the bar top and steel where washdown speed matters most.
