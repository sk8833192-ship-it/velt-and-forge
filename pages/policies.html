01 · Your timeline

Made-to-Order Production
& Shipping Policy

Every custom stamp is made to order. Production begins only after you approve the final artwork proof. An exact estimated ship date is shown before checkout, so you can decide whether the schedule works for you before paying.

Your estimate depends on proof approval and the workshop schedule. It is an estimated dispatch date, not a guaranteed arrival date. We do not promise a 48-hour turnaround. If proof changes affect your production slot, we will confirm a revised estimate with you.

Shipping destinations, delivery options and any shipping charges are shown before payment. Tracking is emailed when your order is dispatched. Carrier transit times and any destination customs processing are separate from production time.

For a wedding, event or trade opening, email orders@veltandforge.com before ordering so we can review your date against the current schedule.

02 · Approval & care

Custom Proof Approval,
Defects & Returns Policy

You approve the final artwork proof before machining begins. Check the spelling, layout, dimensions and the design shown on that proof; the mirrored CNC file is the manufacturing version of your approved mark.

Cancellation is free anytime before proof approval and machining. If you have already paid, a cancellation at that stage receives a full refund. Contact orders@veltandforge.com with your order ID as soon as you wish to cancel.

Custom-engraved dies are made to your approved artwork and cannot be resold. Once machining begins, we do not accept non-defective returns or change-of-mind cancellations of the custom die.

If the machining differs from your approved proof, your stamp arrives damaged, or the alloy fails the specification published for your order, we will provide a full replacement or refund. Email your order ID, a description of the issue and any useful photographs; we will arrange the appropriate next steps, including any necessary return.

These terms do not limit any consumer rights that apply by law.

03 · Your mark stays yours

Privacy
& Artwork Handling

Your uploaded logo stays in your browser during free Studio checking. Previewing, checking and downloading a mirrored CNC proof do not send that artwork to our workshop.

Artwork is transmitted only when you submit an order. We store submitted artwork strictly to machine your stamp and support that order. We do not sell your logo or publish it as an example without your permission.

Order processing uses the contact and shipping details needed to fulfil and support your commission. The payment provider handles your payment details; our order record contains payment references and the information needed to reconcile your order.

For a question about your information, or to request access, correction or deletion of submitted artwork or personal details, email orders@veltandforge.com. Some transaction information may need to be retained to meet legal or accounting obligations.

04 · Speak to us

Terms of Sale
& Contact

Your order is for a custom stamp made to the specification and artwork you approve. Review the selected material, face size, handle, any additional head, total price, shipping details and estimated ship date before paying. Prices are shown in USD; any applicable shipping charges or taxes must be shown before payment.

Please submit only artwork you own or have permission to reproduce. A Studio manufacturability check helps prepare a design; final proof approval is still required before machining.

Order support: orders@veltandforge.com. Include your order ID for questions about a paid order, a proof, shipping, cancellation or a defect.
